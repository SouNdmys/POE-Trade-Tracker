## What changed

The POE2 0.5.5 launch release. Recorded on launch night from in-game screenshots of every
exchange tab, in both languages, and cross-checked against the official exchange history.

- **The POE2 catalogue knows the 0.5.5 exchange.** 691 assets, up from 660. Thirty-one are new:
  the seventeen Jiquani's and Atziri's soul cores, the four Orbs of Sacrifice, Origin Cradle,
  Origin Spark, Origin Core, Call of the Shadows, both Necrotic Catalysts, An Audience with the
  King, Sacred Bloom, Cryptic Key and Liquid Verisium. Twenty-two Traditional Chinese names
  changed in the client (Thaumaturgic Flux is now 熔劑, Head of the King is 國王首級, and so on);
  the old spellings stay as aliases so older screenshots still resolve. Thaumaturgic Flux 5–20
  moved to the Expedition tab. Twelve assets left the exchange (eight omens, Flux 1–4) and are
  kept so history stays readable.
- **Every official exchange path maps.** The path table grew from 648 to 679 entries; the 0.5.5
  league's 654 traded paths all resolve, by count and by volume. The Exchange page, the exchange
  radar and the export therefore see the new assets from the first hour.
- **Pick the league from a list.** The Season page shows a searchable dropdown of every league
  name the last sync saw in the official data, including hardcore and private leagues; choosing
  one saves and restarts the sync. The text box stays for typing a name the data has not shown
  yet.
- **A confirmed-empty hour is not a life sentence.** The official CDN publishes an hour one to
  two hours late, sometimes more, and the program used to freeze any hour still empty after
  three hours as permanently empty. Empty hours are now revisited a day later, for up to a
  week, newest first, four per sync round. A refilled hour also makes its day recompute — a
  bug that existed before: a repaired hour never updated the day line, in the program and in
  the probe's `--audit`. The Exchange page header counts confirmed-empty hours since the
  league's first published hour, and hides the count when it is zero.
- **The exchange radar wants a real baseline before it calls a rise.** In the first days of a
  league the anchor is scarce and a baseline day may hold a handful of trades, which is how
  `+48440943%` got into the band. A rise now needs at least 200 exalted of volume on its
  baseline day. The table's change column is unchanged and still shows the raw number.
- **Two new caveats on the Monitor page, neither blocks anything.** A listing whose stock is
  ten times off the rest of its side is marked as unlike the rest (a misread 1855 → 18550 no
  longer passes silently). A captured book whose best rate is ten times off the pair's recent
  daily median is logged as a suspect identity — the Exalted / Perfect Exalted kind of
  mistake — and stored anyway.

## If you had this configured

- **The catalogue changed, so captures start a fresh context.** Books captured before this
  update stay in the database under the previous context; the pages show the books captured
  after it, and the Analytics "today" column reads both. Nothing is deleted.
- `settings.json` gains `leagues_seen` under each game's `exchange` block, filled by the next
  sync. Every setting you had keeps its meaning.
- Each sync round may make up to four extra requests to recheck empty hours.
- POE2 players: after the first sync the league dropdown will offer `Forbidden Rites`; that is
  the 0.5.5 league's name in the official data.

## Install

Create a folder, extract the zip into it, run `ptt-app.exe`. The zip carries no folder of its
own — twelve files arrive as four loose ones plus `assets\` and `licenses\` — so extracting
straight into Downloads scatters them.

Do not extract into `C:\Program Files`. There is no installer and the program cannot elevate
itself, so the updater will refuse to write there.

Updating from inside the app: press check, then install, then close and reopen the program.
Your settings, calibration and database are untouched.
